Select cells, then click colors to add or remove them. A cell can hold several colors and splits into equal slices. S toggles Shade. Shade (S) and Land (L) drive shading puzzles. The other colors are free markers.

Rules

    Keys: 1–6 enter a digit, C toggles pencil marks, arrows move (Shift extends), Backspace erases, Ctrl+Z undoes. Drag across cells to select several.

    Pick any mix of variants. The generator builds a grid, adds clues from it, then trims them until exactly one solution remains.

    Clues

    . Relaxed keeps more givens, Minimal keeps as few clues as uniqueness allows.

    Shade and Land cells are given parts of the shading solution. The other colors only decorate. Click a painted cell with the same color to clear it.

    Leave blank so the cell’s digit gives the size.
